# Original reading-pattern illustrations

Created for Loop & Stitch on 2026-10-13 as editable SVG diagrams. All sixteen illustrations use our article’s wording, six-stitch practice example and nine-stitch repeat example. No Craft Yarn Council or Leisure Arts diagrams or photographs were copied. The figures are learning schematics and workflow diagrams, not photographs of hands performing stitches.

The article links to the Craft Yarn Council reference for further reading. Its original source illustrations are credited there to Leisure Arts; they are not licensed for our reuse merely by being visible online.

Each section has a caption, alternate text, and a link to view its SVG at full size. The chain schematic deliberately omits the live loop and says so. The double crochet count diagram makes the counting convention explicit.
