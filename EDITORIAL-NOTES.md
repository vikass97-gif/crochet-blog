# Editorial review — 2026-10-12

Seven published guides received a revision of their introductions for specific reader needs and clearer, more natural wording. Duplicate glossary guidance was merged in the pattern-reading guide; help-seeking was moved after troubleshooting. Repeated small-project and time-commitment sections were removed from the first-project guide. A duplicated next-step section was removed from the beginner tutorial.

Technical checks in this pass: US/UK terminology is stated; the ten-stitch exercises specify their own non-counting turning-chain conventions; foundations of 11, 12 and 13 chains produce ten stitches when starting in the second, third and fourth chain respectively. The six-stitch example and increase to nine remain consistent. Gauge examples distinguish stitch and row counts and use explicit measuring spans.

No author biography, personal making experience, tested-pattern claim, publication date, or credential was invented. Content was written and revised with AI assistance. This is an editorial review, not proof that a human crocheter has worked every exercise. Images remain editorial covers, not validated stitch diagrams.
