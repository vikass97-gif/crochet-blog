# Launch and international growth

## Before publication

- The default public origin is https://crochet-blog-26tq.vercel.app. Canonical URLs, the seven-page sitemap, and social image URLs use this origin even when NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL is unset. Set NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL before building if you move to a custom domain.
- Submit /sitemap.xml in Google Search Console after publication. Verify the live canonical tags, robots.txt, indexability, and structured data.
- Check mobile layouts in a browser and measure Core Web Vitals on the deployed site. No production performance score has been measured yet.
- Add verified author information and real publication/update dates when available. These are deliberately not invented in the article schema.

## Content and international reach

The current site is English-only and uses US crochet terminology. Its three original guides are preserved. No translations or hreflang alternates are advertised until matching localized pages exist.

Build useful tutorials with original step-by-step photos, stitch demonstrations, tested patterns, and clear explanations of US/UK terminology. Prioritize specific reader questions and search intent rather than a target word count. Add accurate contributor biographies and cite sources where useful.

For additional languages, publish complete translated pages at distinct URLs (for example /fr/blog/...), translate navigation and metadata, and add reciprocal hreflang entries and localized sitemap URLs. Choose languages from audience and search data, not assumptions.

Use Search Console to monitor indexing, queries, impressions, and clicks. Compare content performance over time and improve pages that answer a clear reader need. Technical SEO and animations alone do not guarantee rankings or traffic.
